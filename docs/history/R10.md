# R10 — history

**Done, and off the rules list, 2026-09-14.** The pickers that hid the rate number (`Host
ratio` and its kind) were retired with the rate block on 2026-09-04. Checked in every
plugin's `slider_show` lines that day: no number is hidden behind a picker. The three old
pickers left in the files are hidden and do nothing. What still fills in a number leaves it
visible: the note name pickers, and Womb's `Set breath rate`. Rozaya agreed to take it off.

## The rule as it stood, verbatim

## R10. The value stays visible; a picker jumps to a value and gets out of the way

The suite's convenience pickers (`Host ratio`, `Breath rate`, the pan-speed pickers)
were designed to *write a value and get out of the way*. In practice they do the
opposite: **13 plugins hide the rate slider whenever the picker is on anything but
`Custom`** — `slider_show(slider1, rate_mode != 1 || sliderN == 0)`.

**Rule: the value slider is always visible. The picker is a jump-to, never a gate.**

- The picker writes the value and is done. It never controls whether the value can be
  seen or reached.
- **When the value no longer matches what the picker names, the picker snaps back to
  `Custom`.** Without it the picker becomes a label that lies -- the `infantile.RPP` hunt,
  where a hidden picker stamped `0.5` over a hand-set rate.
