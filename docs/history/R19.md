## How it was decided, choice by choice

Read from the six plugins (Tremolo, both sweeping filters, Melody, Polyrhythm v3, Rhythm
Track), not the docs. Every description below was read from the code; none was run yet.

- **Accent L / Weak R was filed under "still" and it is not.** Rhythm Track's `beat_pan` puts
  beat 0 left and every other beat right, so it moves every beat. It went into the jumping
  group, straight after the Alternating family. Rozaya: *"I think we should keep it after
  alternating. it's a nice transition into the rest"*.
- **Linked Sweep leads the glides**, because it rides the plugin's own cycle as the jumping
  choices do. Rozaya: *"It should lead. Not like I've been able to figure out how to use those
  anyway, the multiplier is the off-putter there"*. The installed control already reads
  `Pan sweep every (cycles)`, not a multiplier; what remains is that it counts only the slow
  way, so a sweep faster than the cycle needs 0.5 or 0.25. Filed in the backlog.
- **Every plugin gains the choices that fit it.** Rozaya: *"Yes. why not"*. Claude first
  ruled Spread out of single-sound plugins; Rozaya pushed back (*"Spread is arguably the
  easiest out of the lot"*), then, told that the filters' Distributed already steps the sound
  left to right one cycle at a time: *"I forgot about distributed. no, spread then is a
  polyrhythm thing"*.
- **Flip is a switch, not a choice.** Rhythm Track's `Pan direction {Normal, Flipped}`
  multiplies every choice's position by -1. The filters' three `(Flipped)` choices were read
  and each is an exact mirror of its partner, so a switch does the same job and flips every
  choice. Rozaya: *"No, I like your idea. I haven't used it in so long I didn't know it was
  there"*.
- **Melody's `Tremolo` pan choice follows no tremolo.** Melody has none; every voice swings
  together at `Pan base rate`. Polyrhythm's Tremolo swings each voice at its own tremolo rate.
  It was to be renamed Sway (*"Yeah, sway fits."*), but Sway is Increment with the increment
  at zero, so Rozaya dropped it from both Melody and Polyrhythm: *"Leave it out heh"*. Sway
  stays for the four plugins with no Increment.

## What the mess actually is

Every plugin's Pan Mode list grew by appending, so each one is in the order its
features happened to arrive rather than any order a person could reason about.

- **Polyrhythm** now reads: Tremolo, Increment, Spread, Spread Reversed,
  Alternating, Alternating (Flipped), Distributed, Distributed (Flipped),
  Distributed (Ping-pong), Converging, Converging (Ping-pong), Diverging,
  Diverging (Ping-pong), **Alternating every 2, every 4, every 8**. The three
  extra Alternating variants are at 13-15, with the whole Distributed /
  Converging / Diverging family sitting between them and the Alternating they
  belong to. That was done on 2026-09-02 and it is the clearest example.
- **Full Feature Tremolo** has no `Alternating (Flipped)`; both sweeping filters
  do.
- **rhythm-track** has a shorter list in a different order, plus `Accent L /
  Weak R` which exists nowhere else.
- The oscillator plugins lead with four modes (Tremolo / Increment / Spread /
  Spread Reversed) that no effect plugin has.

## Proposed order — group by WHAT THE PAN DOES

Three groups, in this order, each plugin including only the members it has:

1. **Still** — the pan does not move on its own.
   `Mono` · `Spread` · `Spread Reversed` · `Accent L / Weak R`
2. **Stepped** — the pan moves one position per cycle / note. Two-position
   members first, then multi-position.
   `Alternating` · `Alternating (Flipped)` · `Alternating every 2` ·
   `Alternating every 4` · `Alternating every 8` · `Distributed` ·
   `Distributed (Flipped)` · `Distributed (Ping-pong)` · `Converging` ·
   `Converging (Ping-pong)` · `Diverging` · `Diverging (Ping-pong)`
3. **Continuous** — the pan travels on a clock of its own.
   `Tremolo` · `Increment` · `Pan Sweep` · `Pan Sweep (Flipped)` ·
   `Linked Sweep`

The grouping is the useful part: **still / stepped / continuous** is the
distinction a listener actually hears, and it tells you immediately whether a
mode can drift against the material (only group 3 can).
