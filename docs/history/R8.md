## The two drafts this rule replaced, moved out of the plan 2026-09-09

**Draft one said the opposite of the rule** — that a slider should land in
roughly 100-1000 positions, and that a million positions meant the precision was
fictional.

**Draft two said you can only arrow in the parameter list, never type.**
CORRECTED 2026-08-31 by Star: focus a parameter, Tab, and there is an editable
value field; and in the FX dialog you can type into the box beside the slider.

Both drafts were arguing about how far you have to TRAVEL to reach a value. The
rule that survived is about which values EXIST at all, which is a different
question and the one that matters. That reasoning is live and stayed in the
plan.
