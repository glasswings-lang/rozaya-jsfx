## How it was closed — and the mistake in the middle of it

The first pass classified a target by asking whether the plugin re-reads it every sample.
By that test Tremolo, the Sweeping Filter, Sweep Dwell and Sustain Looper were all
"continuous" and needed nothing. Rozaya: *"Some of those do have their own cycles"* — and
that is the whole point. **Re-reading a per-occurrence value every sample IS the bug R23
names, not evidence against it.** A value that belongs to an occurrence must be latched
when the occurrence begins; recomputing it mid-occurrence reshapes the thing you are
already hearing.

Sweep Dwell is the clearest demonstration and the one to test against. Its four segment
lengths are drift targets, and `src/sweep-dwell-filter.jsfx:641-648` rebuilds all four
boundaries every sample from a single 0..1 phase. Drifting `High dwell length` moves the
edges of the segment while the playhead is inside it, so the cycle stretches and squashes
underneath rather than the NEXT cycle coming out different.

## What was read, 2026-09-15

All 19 plugin sources, at the drift block and at every consumer of a drift offset.

- **Already have `Drift movement (per target)`, 8:** Breath Gen, Bubbler, Dapple, Heartbeat,
  Melody, Rhythm Track, Passage (`per slot and target`), Womb.
- **Owe it, 7:** Tremolo, the Sweeping Filter, Sweep Dwell, Polyrhythm v3, Shepard Scale,
  Shepard Tone, the Morpher (grain length). In each, an envelope or segment value is
  recomputed from a running phase every sample rather than latched at the event.
- **Owe nothing, 3:** Veil, Resonance Bank, the Stereo Phaser — continuous filters with no
  event to wait for. Resonance Bank is the proof by construction: it has no cycle, so its
  `Drift period mode` offers BPM/Seconds/Hz/beats and no `Cycles` at all.
- **Left alone by decision, 1:** Sustain Looper. It has a loop pass, but the region was
  deliberately built to move live and crossfade (`src/sustain_looper.jsfx:388-397`), which
  is the Bubbler/Dapple *"The stepping was deliberately live"* case.

## The second half, found while reading

`Cycles` in a multi-speed plugin counts the MASTER rate's cycles, for every target.
Polyrhythm v3 (`drift_phase_inc_base = (rate_val_hz * combined_scale) / srate`, line 1607)
and Shepard Tone (`drift_cycle_rate`, line ~869) both have eight voices at eight rates, and
a per-voice target drifted "every 8 cycles" counts somebody else's eight. This is the
literal reading of Rozaya's *"drift this thing every two cycles and drift this thing every
four"*, and it is why the rule now says `Cycles` counts THAT target's cycles.
