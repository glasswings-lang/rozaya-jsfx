# R3 -- Every target has a slider you can reach

**Done, and off the rules list, 2026-09-14.** Checked against the plugins that day, after the
naming pass: every Drift and Ramp target leads to a slider, or to a picker that shows one.
Breath Generator has its `Breath rate` slider. The one target with no slider of its own,
Womb's, now says what it moves: `Breath rate (all 4 parts together)` -- it stretches the four
breath segments together (Rozaya: *"seems like a more honest name there"*, then *"That works
yeah"*). The six plugins whose targets sit behind a picker were read page by page: Polyrhythm
(Voice), the Morpher (Layer), Breath Generator and Heartbeat (Pitch target) and Sweep Dwell
(Segment) already named theirs; Rhythm Track's page still described its retired beat
frequencies and never named its Pitch target, and was rewritten from the code. Picker targets
are written `V3 gain`, `Layer 2 level`, `Strong pitch`: the picker's entry and the control.

The rule as it stood, verbatim:

## R3. Every target has a slider you can reach

A target list may not offer something the user cannot see or set. Two consequences:

- **Breath Generator gains `Breaths per minute`.** It is the dedicated breathing plugin
  and it currently lets you drift and ramp a rate it gives you no way to set. Womb has
  had this slider since v2.
- Where a target names one entry of a **selector-backed group** (Melody v2's `Voice`
  selector, Morpher's `Layer` selector), the target string is `<selector option> <slider
  label minus parenthetical>` and the plugin page must say it is reached via the
  selector. This is the one legitimate case of a target with no dedicated slider. See
  Open Question 1.
