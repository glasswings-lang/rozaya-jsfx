**Closed, and off the rules list, 2026-09-15.** Nothing was built. The rule itself now lives
in `docs/suite-consistency-plan.md`, Part 2, "The rules inside the order"; what is owed is
one entry in `docs/backlog.md`, folded into each plugin's own amount-unit turn (R26/R27).

The ORDERING half was built on 2026-09-11 for nine plugins (`tools/r24_batch_migrate_20260911.py`
and the per-plugin `*_r24_migrate_*.py`), and current-state lists it as not yet heard. What
closed on 2026-09-15 is the other half: whether every sound-shaping control is IN the list.

## What was measured, 2026-09-15

`tools/r24_target_audit.py`, written for this and kept: it prints every slider in every
plugin beside that plugin's Drift target options and classifies nothing. Two other checks
were run from the same parse:

- **Drift and Ramp share one list in all 19.** Every plugin's two option lists are
  byte-identical, lengths 5 to 88. Nothing owed there.
- **Play for / Rest for.** Fifteen plugins have the controls. Thirteen offer them as
  targets; **the Sweeping Filter and Sweep Dwell do not**. That is a plain gap, settled by
  the rule's own wording, no decision needed. Five plugins have no Play for / Rest for at
  all -- Veil, Womb, Resonance Bank, the Stereo Phaser, Sustain Looper -- which is R27's
  transport work, not this.

## The decision Rozaya made

Seven controls shape the sound but move in WHOLE STEPS, and had been left out for that
reason. Asked whether a drift that jumps rather than glides should be offered at all,
Rozaya: *"Yeah, I think they should be in the picker for drift, and ramp, for that
matter."* So they go in:

- Melody `Octave shift` (-4..4) and Polyrhythm v3 `Octave shift` (-4..4)
- Shepard Scale `Centre octave` (0..8) and Shepard Tone `Centre octave` (0..8)
- the Morpher `Layer harmonics` (0..64, per layer)

The reasoning offered against, and rejected: a drift on one of these sits still and then
jumps an octave. Refusing it decides for Rozaya what is worth trying, and that is the
failure `docs/history/working-practice.md` keeps recording.

**Structural counts stay out**, as the rule says: `Octave count` on both Shepards,
`Stages` on the Stereo Phaser, `Voices (ensemble)` on Sustain Looper, `Cycle steps`,
Resonance Bank's `Band selector`, the Morpher's and Passage's `Capture point` and
`Capture average`.

## Still open, raised and not yet answered

Three smooth controls that are not targets anywhere: `Source fine tune` (the Morpher and
Passage, -1000..1000 semitones), `Phase offset` (Polyrhythm v3) and `LFO start phase`
(the Sweeping Filter, -180..180 degrees). The last two are set-once-at-the-start values,
so they may belong with Start delay rather than with the targets.
