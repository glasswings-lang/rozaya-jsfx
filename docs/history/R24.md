## What was measured, 2026-09-15

`tools/r24_target_audit.py`, written for this and kept: it prints every slider in every
plugin beside that plugin's Drift target options and classifies nothing. Two other checks
were run from the same parse:

- **Play for / Rest for.** Fifteen plugins have the controls. Thirteen offer them as

## The decision Rozaya made

Seven controls shape the sound but move in WHOLE STEPS, and had been left out for that
reason. Asked whether a drift that jumps rather than glides should be offered at all,
Rozaya: *"Yeah, I think they should be in the picker for drift, and ramp, for that
matter."* So they go in:

- Melody `Octave shift` (-4..4) and Polyrhythm v3 `Octave shift` (-4..4)
- Shepard Scale `Centre octave` (0..8) and Shepard Tone `Centre octave` (0..8)
- the Morpher `Layer harmonics` (0..64, per layer)

Asked whether 104 entries on the Morpher's picker is workable, Rozaya: *"it shows
0-highest number so if I need to I can just tap at it."* **A picker is typed, not arrowed,
so option count is not a constraint** -- the runner compiles and sets a 256-entry picker,
and `tools/bridge_ui_test.py` proved 87 live in REAPER on 2026-09-11.

The reasoning offered against, and rejected: a drift on one of these sits still and then
jumps an octave. Refusing it decides for Rozaya what is worth trying, and that is the
failure `docs/history/working-practice.md` keeps recording.

**Structural counts stay out**, as the rule says: `Octave count` on both Shepards,
`Stages` on the Stereo Phaser, `Voices (ensemble)` on Sustain Looper, `Cycle steps`,
Resonance Bank's `Band selector`, the Morpher's and Passage's `Capture point` and
`Capture average`.

## The start-only controls, answered 2026-09-15

Three smooth controls were not targets anywhere. `Source fine tune` (the Morpher and
Passage, -1000..1000 semitones) had simply been overlooked and goes in.

`Phase offset` (Polyrhythm v3) and `LFO start phase` (the Sweeping Filter, -180..180
degrees) stay OUT. Both say where in the cycle the thing begins and are read once when it
starts, which puts them with Start delay. Rozaya: *"There's no reason to drift a
start-only control."* **That is the test to apply to the next one of these: not whether
the control is smooth, but whether the engine reads it again after the start.**
