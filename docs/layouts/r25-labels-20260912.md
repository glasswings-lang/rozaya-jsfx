R25 (`docs/suite-consistency-plan.md`): a control behind a selector ends its name with
which kind it is. Rozaya: *"if it's gonna do that, can't it at least say that's what it's
doing?"* and *"anything would do at this point"*.

**Where every kind below comes from:** `tools/selector_scope_probe.py`, run live in REAPER
on `claude-testing002-bridge.RPP` through the kin bridge, 2026-09-12 -- set on one option,
switch, read back. 314 measurements on the full run plus a Resonance Bank rerun that also
probed `Band selector (0-15)` (missed on the full run). Nothing unclear. The installed
plugins matched `src/` for all 17 files (Passage differs and is not in this list: its
labels wait for its rebuild's renames stage).

**Built by** `tools/r25_build_list.py` from that measurement, then **read line by line by
hand before applying**; the hand read caught Heartbeat missing (a space in its file name),
fixed before this list was written. **Applied by** `tools/r25_rename_apply.py` (exact old
label on the exact slider, or refusal). A rename moves no saved value; no migration.

Pattern found in all 16 plugins with one target selector: amounts, period, period mode,
shape, movement, play for, rest for, Ramp by, duration and start delay are per target;
Drift period unit, Ramp time unit and Ramp engage (and the Morpher's Drift restart) serve
all targets. Resonance Bank's per-target controls are also per band. The Morpher's Capture
slot and Layer and Polyrhythm's Voice were measured: no Drift or Ramp control switches
with them.
